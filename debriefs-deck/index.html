The method

The 2023 board taught a full root cause toolkit

RCA root cause analysis

Find the cause behind the symptom before fixing anything.

5 why form of RCA

Take each Challenge and ask and Answer Why? 5 times. You make make forks if there are more than 1 answer to why

Ishikawa/fishbone and 6m

Categorize your Challenges or Lessons Learned into Man Method Machines Movement Measurement Mother Nature.

SWOT analysis

Which root causes of success are at risk of going away next year? Do you have a student graduating?

Risk Matrices

Assign a number 1-5 for Impact of issue, and one for Feasibility of implementation. The multiplied number is your Priority
