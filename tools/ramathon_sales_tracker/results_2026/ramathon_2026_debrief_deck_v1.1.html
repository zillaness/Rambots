2 · What happened

Dumpling and Salmon nigiri sold fastest

Creeper is the story

Third-fastest seller with less than half the stock of the top two, so it was gone by 11:48 AM.

How rate works

Units sold per hour while the design was on the table. One sale counts at most 3 of a design, so the parent who bought 8 Dumplings doesn't skew it.

Slow isn't always unwanted

We printed different amounts, and on a crowded table some designs may not have been noticed. LEGO sold twice in two minutes, then never again.

2 · What happened

Busy at the start, slow at lunch

Same shape as past years

A rush once the doors open, a cool-down, then sometimes a smaller bump near the end.

Lunch is food time

We've never sold food. At meal times people buy food instead of activities or items, so lunch is always our slow stretch.

The last half hour

Other booths blow out their food or give things away free, which pulls kids away and muddies the numbers.

3 · Why

Copters fell about 95% from last year

−95%

5 kits for 27 tickets (about 24 net). Last year: about 450 net at the same prices. 2 of the 5 were toroidal.

Guess 1 · Clickers pulled the same kids

Clickers took 94% of the sales table's tickets.

Test: give copters their own table, or run a flying demo.

Guess 2 · Families already own one (most likely)

After two strong years, many returning families have a copter at home.

Test: ask at the table, or sell replacement props and ripcords instead of full kits.

Still in the bin

88 launchers · 155 propellers · 42 toroidal propellers · 100 ripcords
