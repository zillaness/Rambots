Units sold per hour while the design was on the table. One sale counts at most 3 of a design, so the parent who bought 8 Dumplings doesn't skew it.

2 · What happened

Busy at the start, slow at lunch

Same shape as past years

A rush once the doors open, a cool-down, then sometimes a smaller bump near the end.

Lunch is food time

We've never sold food. At meal times people buy food instead of activities or items, so lunch is always our slow stretch.

The last half hour

Other booths blow out their food or give things away free, which pulls kids away and muddies the numbers.

3 · Why

−95%
