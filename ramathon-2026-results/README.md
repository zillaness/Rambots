# Ram-a-thon 2026 Results

| Page | Link |
|---|---|
| Interactive sales chart | https://zillaness.github.io/Rambots/ramathon-2026-results/sales-chart.html |
| Same chart, opened on a view | `#total`, `#rate` or `#gap` at the end of the link; add `-clickers` (e.g. `#rate-clickers`) for clickers only, or `-booth` (e.g. `#total-booth`) to add the Plinko, Battle and blowout estimates |

Four views: per design, combined total, tickets per hour and minutes per sale. The combined views compare the actual rolling 30-minute pace with the day average (gross on the axis, net after CCA's 10% on hover), for the whole sales table or clickers only, and show the whole-booth total counted at the end of the day. For the whole table, a toggle stacks Plinko, Battle and the blowout on top as a hatched estimate band (none of them have timestamps). In the per-design view each dot is one sale: hover or tap it for the time, what was in the sale and its tickets. Hover a line to highlight it, click up to two lines (or their chips) to compare them, and toggle the dotted "if we had enough stock" projections for the three designs that sold out early.

The page is unlisted (not on the Rambots site's landing page) and asks search engines not to index it. The URL is still public, so anyone with the link can open it.

## Where it comes from

Do not edit `sales-chart.html` here. It is a copy of `ramathon_2026_clicker_sales_curve_v1.5.html`, generated by `plot_clicker_sales_curve_v1.5.py` in `tools/ramathon_sales_tracker/results_2026/` on the `ramathon-carnival` branch. Edit the script, regenerate, and copy the new page over this one. The photos in `infographic_photos/` come from the same folder (`crop_infographic_photos_v1.0.py`).

The infographic and the full workbook are not published here on purpose. They hold internal estimates, including other groups' numbers. The chart shows only Rambots' own totals. The chart also leaves out buyer age and gender.
