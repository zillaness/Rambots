# Ram-a-thon 2026 Results

| Page | Link |
|---|---|
| Interactive clicker sales chart | https://zillaness.github.io/Rambots/ramathon-2026-results/sales-chart.html |

Hover a line to highlight it, click up to two lines (or their chips) to compare them, and toggle the dotted "if we had enough stock" projections for the three designs that sold out early.

The page is unlisted (not on the Rambots site's landing page) and asks search engines not to index it. The URL is still public, so anyone with the link can open it.

## Where it comes from

Do not edit `sales-chart.html` here. It is a copy of `ramathon_2026_clicker_sales_curve_v1.2.html`, generated by `plot_clicker_sales_curve_v1.2.py` in `tools/ramathon_sales_tracker/results_2026/` on the `ramathon-carnival` branch. Edit the script, regenerate, and copy the new page over this one. The photos in `infographic_photos/` come from the same folder (`crop_infographic_photos_v1.0.py`).

The infographic and the full workbook are not published here on purpose. They hold internal estimates, including other groups' numbers.
