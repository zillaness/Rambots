# Lam Research Giving Programs — Mentor Packet

## Draft email to send

> **Subject: Lam volunteer hours turn into money for the team (there's a 90-day clock)**
>
> Three Lam programs pay out to Rambots, and all three run through you rather than through us. We can't claim any of them on our own.
>
> 1. **Volunteer grant.** Lam donates $10 for every hour you volunteer, up to $2,000 a year. You aren't being paid anything; the check goes to the team. A season of build nights and competitions clears that cap without trying.
> 2. **Matching gift.** Lam matches your donations dollar for dollar, also up to $2,000 a year.
> 3. **Inspire.Together.** Any full-time employee can nominate a nonprofit and submit a grant application on its behalf. This is the one that matters most. Lam Foundation grants are otherwise invitation only, and the foundation's two focus areas are K-12 STEM/Education and Quality of Life. We are exactly what they fund.
>
> The catch on 1 and 2 is a 90-day submission window from the date of the donation or the volunteer activity. Hours logged late are worth nothing, so the log has to start on day one of the season rather than at the end of it.
>
> What I need from you:
>
> A. Log into Lam's giving portal and tell me which platform it runs on, and whether the $2,000 caps on 1 and 2 are separate or a single combined $2,000.
> B. Start a volunteer hour log when the season opens.
> C. Tell me whether Inspire.Together is currently open and what it needs from our side.
>
> I'll send you our 501(c)(3) determination letter, which all three require.
>
> One more thing worth knowing: Lam is a title sponsor of FIRST Global and has pledged $10 million to FIRST. You aren't asking them to fund something new. You're asking them to extend something they already believe in to a team eight miles from their own headquarters.

## Terms as currently understood

| Program | Rate / cap | Who triggers it | Who receives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volunteer grant | $10 per hour, up to $2,000 per year per full-time employee | The employee logs hours and submits | The 501(c)(3) |
| Matching gift | Dollar for dollar, up to $2,000 per year per full-time employee | The employee donates, then submits the match request | The 501(c)(3) |
| Inspire.Together | Amount not published | The employee nominates and applies on the org's behalf | The 501(c)(3) |

Eligible organizations include K-12 educational institutions, health and human services, arts and cultural organizations, civic and community organizations, and environmental organizations.

Requests must reach Lam within **90 days** of the contribution or the completion of the volunteer activity. A copy of the 501(c)(3) tax exemption letter is required.

## Two things still unresolved

**Are the caps separate or combined?** Published summaries describe the matching cap as "$2,000 per year" and then say volunteer time is "also capped at a total of $2,000 per year." That wording is ambiguous. If the caps are independent, one mentor is worth up to $4,000 a year before Inspire.Together enters the picture. One email to Community Affairs settles it.

**Which platform?** Lam runs employee giving through a CSR platform where employees submit matching gifts and log volunteer hours, but which one (Benevity, YourCause, or something else) could not be confirmed externally. The mentor will see it the moment they log in.

## The part worth repeating

The caps are **per employee, per year**. Every Lam employee connected to the team carries their own $2,000 matching cap and their own $2,000 volunteer cap. Any Lam parent in the CCA community counts, not only the mentor.

The volunteer grant also has no technical requirement attached to it. A Lam parent who chaperones a competition, runs concessions, or helps clean up after a build session generates $10 an hour exactly the same as an engineering mentor does.

So the next move after this email is a parent survey. Two Lam parents alongside the mentor could out-earn most of the national grants on the target list, for a fraction of the writing.
