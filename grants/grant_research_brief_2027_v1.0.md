# Grant Opportunity Research Brief — 2027 Season

**Team:** Rambots FRC 2204 · California Crosspoint Academy · 25500 Industrial Blvd, Hayward CA 94545 (Alameda County)
**Compiled:** 29 July 2026
**Companion file:** `rambots_sponsor_grant_target_list_v1.5.xlsx`

## Where the season sits

It is late July 2026, which puts the team in the pre-season window for the 2027 FRC season. This is
when the fall corporate grant cycle opens and when most of the year's non-relationship money is
decided. The community Grant Database that was supplied is national in scope and was last maintained
around the 2025-26 season, so its dates are a year stale and its geography skews East Coast. Every
date below was re-verified against current sources.

## The two things that are time-critical

**1. Intuitive Foundation — closes the second week of August 2026.**
Roughly two weeks out. Eligibility is registration with FIRST for the current season, non-rookie
status, and at least one official FIRST competition — Rambots clears all three. Grants run about
$1,000–$2,000, with notification in the third week of October and payment through FIRST in November.
Intuitive Surgical is a Sunnyvale company, so local proximity is a legitimate angle to lead with.
Contact `FIRST@intuitive-foundation.org`.

*Caveat: the foundation's own page could not be loaded directly from this environment, so the exact
close date needs a short confirmation on the site before relying on it.*

**2. Dow — open now, first-come-first-served.**
The correction worth acting on. FRC teams can earn up to $5,000. The program is aimed at teams with a
Dow employee mentor or in a Dow community, but teams without a mentor may still apply, and as of 23
June 2026 those applications are being reviewed. Because awards are first-come-first-served until
funds run out, delay costs money directly. Portal: `first.dowstem.us/access`.

## New Bay Area leads

The largest gap in both source files is local funders. These are not on either sheet.

- **PG&E Corporation Foundation / DreamMakers Fund** — the best new fit found. Team grants for FTC and
  FRC teams inside PG&E's Northern California service territory, administered through FIRST
  California. Hayward is squarely inside that territory. The foundation has given $175,000 to FIRST
  supporting more than 16,000 California students a year. Route: the FIRST California Regional
  Director via `cafirst.org/grants/`.
- **Synopsys Outreach Foundation, STEM Opportunity Fund** — restricted to Greater Bay Area K-12, which
  is exactly where the team sits. Free STEM supplies, teacher training, and project/event grants,
  running each fall.
- **Applied Materials Foundation** — Northern California is a named priority region. Three cycles a
  year (roughly January, June, September), grants from $1,000 to $1,000,000, STEM education focus with
  a stated interest in girls in STEM. Contact `Applied_Materials_Foundation@AMAT.com`.
- **Clorox Company Foundation** — Oakland HQ, funds STEM education and youth development, administered
  through the East Bay Community Foundation.
- **East Bay Community Foundation** — the hub through which several East Bay corporate funders route
  giving. Registering a profile at `eastbaycf.smapply.io` opens more than one door.
- **Kaiser Permanente, Greater Southern Alameda Area** — their own service-area map names Hayward.
  Youth-development framing lands better here than an engineering pitch.

## Ruled out — stop spending time on these

- **Harbor Freight Tools for Schools Prize** — US public high schools only, and the 2026 cycle closed
  1 May. CCA is private, so it is ineligible regardless of timing.
- **Lam Research Core Values Scholarship** — Fremont Unified and Livermore Valley students only, and
  it is a student scholarship rather than a team grant.
- **Golden State Community Foundation (Warriors)** — funds Alameda and San Francisco counties, but
  does not accept unsolicited proposals.
- **Broadcom Foundation** — no published application process; preselected organizations only.
- **NASA Robotics Alliance Project** — Rookie, New Veteran, and Year-Two teams only. This confirms the
  caution already in the target list; it is worth asking Quinn which program the lead actually meant.
- **John Deere** — requires a confirmed current John Deere employee mentor.
- **Digital Citizen Fund** and **Argosy Foundation via FIRST California** — rookie teams only.
- **Toyota** — its eligible-state list excludes California.

## Two answers to open questions in the tracker

**Open Question #3, "What is FabWorks?"** — Fabworks is an online sheet metal laser cutting and
bending service, not a local machine shop. It runs a formal two-tier sponsorship program for FRC,
FTC, BattleBots, and Formula SAE teams, offering priority laser cutting, partner discount codes, and
social promotion. It belongs in the Custom Fab / Laser Cut competitor group, which makes that group a
three-way pick-one alongside OSH Cut and SendCutSend rather than a two-way. Of the three, Fabworks has
the most explicit FIRST-facing program.

**Student-facing money worth flagging separately** — the PG&E Better Together STEM Scholarship awards
$350,000 across up to 60 students at $10,000 / $5,000 / $2,500, open to seniors in PG&E service
territory enrolling in undergraduate STEM at an accredited four-year California institution or an
HBCU. Deadline is around 12 March. Not team funding, but real money for Rambots seniors and a strong
Impact Award narrative. Flag it to the senior class in the fall.

## Underused asset

The Hayward Industrial Park vendor sheet already in the tracker is the strongest untapped resource in
either file. In-kind machining from a shop two blocks away returns more per hour of effort than most
national grant applications, and it tends to convert into mentorship rather than a one-time check.
Dongar Technologies sits at 25509 Industrial Blvd — essentially next door to CCA at 25500.
